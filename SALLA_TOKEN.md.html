Design principle: Pi first, ST as a benefit - **Checkout methods are Pi only.** The two payment methods are the internal Pi balance and a direct Pi payment through the Pi SDK. - **ST never replaces Pi as a payment method.** At checkout, ST only shows up as an **ST gift card discount**: the card's value is converted at the live ST/Pi rate and subtracted from the Pi total. - **Platform commission applies to the Pi part only.** There is no commission on amounts covered by ST cards, which keeps ST a pure benefit for buyers and merchants. - **Merchants opt in.** Each merchant decides whether to accept ST gift cards on their products. - **Every ST feature can be switched off.** Admin switches control each one (checkout, deposits, withdrawals, transfers), so ST can be paused at any time without affecting Pi payments. ### 3. Security and controls - **Server-side money moves.** All balance changes run in database functions with row-level security. The client never sets balances. - **Re-checked discounts.** Checkout discounts are re-validated on the server against the live card and rate before a Pi payment is approved. - **On-chain deposit checks.** Deposits are verified on Horizon: asset code, issuer, successful transaction and the user's muxed id. - **Protected withdrawals.** Withdrawals need a security PIN, charge a configurable fee and are reconciled against the chain. - **Admin switches:** - ST system on or off - ST at checkout - ST deposits (automatic and by transaction hash) - ST withdrawals and fee - Internal transfers and fee ### 6.
